Law Firms
Today

Medical records arrive by email across 20 cases. A paralegal downloads, renames and files each one, then chases whatever is missing.

With AI

AI renames the records and files them in the right case folder, flags missing records, and drafts a status update to the client for attorney review.

Contractors
Today

A customer asks, "What will a new kitchen cost?" You measure, drive back, dig through price sheets, and the estimate goes out days later.

With AI

You send your measurements and photos (or a voice note) from the truck. AI drafts the estimate from your own prices and markups. You check it and send it the same day.

Trades
Today

A customer calls at 7 PM because their AC is out. It goes to voicemail, and they call the next company.

With AI

AI drafts a reply with your real diagnostic fee and your next open service window. You approve it from your phone and it's sent.
